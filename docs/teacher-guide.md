# Teacher guide — Dove Lesson Studio

For `@Dove Teacher`, `@Dove Reading Host` and anyone who makes session material. Plain English;
share this page in `📝・application-info` or pin it in the teachers' channel.

## What it does

You type the content of your session. The studio makes:

- **Slides** for the Dove Classroom (16:9, same size as the old decks), and
- a **Handout** (A4) for `📂・lesson-resources` — short, easy to read on a phone, answers at the end.

Everything is in Dove's colours and layout. You never move boxes.

## Your first lesson (10 minutes)

1. Open the studio and click **Try the example** to see a finished lesson, or **New lesson**.
2. In **Lesson settings and cover**: write the title in every language, choose the language you
   teach, up to two translation languages, and the level. Session number, date and your Discord
   name are optional — they go on the postmark.
3. On the left, **Add a block** for each part of your session: vocabulary, example sentences, a
   dialogue, an exercise… The preview on the right updates as you type.
4. Use the **⋮** menu next to a block to move, duplicate or delete it.
5. Click **Slides PDF** or **Handout PDF**. A print window opens: choose **Save as PDF**. For
   slides, also turn on **Background graphics**.

## Blocks

| Block | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Objectives | What students will learn today |
| Discussion question | One big question to start talking |
| Cultural note | A short paragraph about culture |
| Vocabulary | Words with translations — slides fill up automatically |
| Example sentences | Sentences with translations |
| Word spotlight | One word or slang: meaning, example, a usage note |
| Abbreviations | Short forms: `vc → você → you` |
| Text and image | A paragraph next to a picture |
| Image gallery | Up to four pictures per slide, with captions |
| Break slide | A big title, like "Exercise time" |
| Fill in the blank | Sentences with gaps; answer key slide is made for you |
| Dialogue | A conversation; each translation gets its own slides |
| Closing | Thank you + next steps |

### Fill in the blank

Put the answer in square brackets: `Eu vou [postar] uma foto.`
More than one correct answer: `[postar/curtir/compartilhar]`.
The word bank is made from your answers unless you type your own.

## Good habits

- **Type each fact once.** The studio repeats it on slides and handout for you — so a typo is
  fixed everywhere at once.
- **Pictures:** use your own screenshots and photos, not stock 3D people. Every picture needs a
  short description (for students who use a screen reader); the studio won't make PDFs until
  each one has one.
- **No emoji on slides** — they look different on every computer. Write `#lesson-resources`.
- **"Too much text on slide N"** means a slide is too full. Shorten the text, or duplicate the
  block and split the items between the two copies.
- **Night** (in Lesson settings) makes dark slides — nicer when you screen-share at night.

## Where is my lesson saved?

**Only in this browser, on this computer.** Nothing is uploaded. So:

- Click **Lesson file** to download a `.dove.json` copy. Keep it, or upload it to
  `📂・lesson-resources` next to the PDF so another teacher can open and reuse it.
- On another computer, use **Open a lesson file** on the start page.
- Clearing your browser data deletes drafts that you haven't downloaded.
